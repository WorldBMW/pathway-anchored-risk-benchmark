!Series_summary = Head and neck squamous cell carcinoma is the 6th most common malignancy worldwide. We performed spatial transcriptomics to profile intra- and inter-tumor heterogeneity. We identified recurring patterns of spatial gene expression that co-localize in subsets of tumors.
!Series_overall_design = Human tumor samples were collected from patients undergoing surgery for head and neck squamous cell carcinoma. Samples were frozen in OCT, sectioned to 10 microns, and processed according to the 10X Visium Spatial Gene Expression protocol.
!Series_overall_design = Human tumor samples were collected from patients undergoing surgery for head and neck squamous cell carcinoma. Samples were were dissociated using the Miltenyi Human Tumor Dissociation Kit, cleaned with ACK RBC lysis, and sorted with CD45+ beads. Cells were loaded onto the 10X chip with a ratio of 70% CD45- and 30% CD45+ cells. cDNA libraries were prepared according to the 10X Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 3' Reagent Kits v3.1 protocol.
!Series_overall_design = Human tumor FFPE samples from patients who underwent surgery for head and neck squamous cell carcinoma were reviewed for tumor purity. Samples with DV200 values >30% were cut to 5 micron sections on slides according to the 10X Demonstrated Protocol. Regions of interest were placed in the tissue slide cassette, probe hybridization and ligation were performed and then slides were loaded onto the CytAssist instrument, per protocol. Samples were aligned to grch38 reference genome and 16 micron bin sizes were used for each sample.
